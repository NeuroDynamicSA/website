_Last Updated: November 15, 2025_

Welcome to **NeuroDynamic** (“we”, “our”, “us”). We are a Saudi-owned technology consultancy committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)** of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you visit our website **NeuroDynamic.sa**, interact with our services, or contact us.

---

#### Scope of This Privacy Policy

This policy applies to:

- Visitors of our website  
- Individuals who contact us via forms, email, phone, or uploaded documents  
- Potential and existing clients engaging with our online materials  

This policy does **not** cover data collected under formal service contracts, which are governed by their own confidentiality and data-protection terms.

---

#### Information We Collect

We collect the minimum necessary information to operate our website, communicate with you, and improve your user experience.

##### Information You Provide Directly
- Name  
- Email address  
- Phone number  
- Company name and role  
- Messages sent through our contact forms  
- Files or documents you voluntarily upload  

##### Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our Site, we may collect:

- IP address  
- Browser type and version  
- Device information  
- Pages visited and duration  
- Referring website  
- Cookies and analytics data  

These are collected using standard web analytics technologies.

---

#### How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

- **Responding to inquiries** and communicating with you  
- **Providing services**, consultations, or assessments you request  
- **Improving website performance** and user experience  
- **Security monitoring** and fraud prevention  
- **Compliance with Saudi regulations and legal obligations**  

We do **not** sell personal data to third parties.

---

#### Legal Basis for Processing (Saudi PDPL)

We process personal data based on:

- Your **explicit consent**  
- Our need to **respond to your requests**  
- Our **legitimate interest** in improving services and ensuring security  
- **Compliance with legal/regulatory obligations** in the Kingdom  

---

#### Data Sharing

We only share your data when necessary:

- **Service providers** (e.g., hosting, analytics, email delivery)  
- **Government authorities** when legally required  
- **Internal teams** to deliver requested services  

We do not share your data for advertising or marketing by third parties.

---

#### Data Storage & International Transfers

We prioritize storing and processing data **within Saudi Arabia**, aligned with Vision 2030’s digital sovereignty goals.

If data must be transferred outside the Kingdom (e.g., for cloud hosting), we ensure:

- Adequate security and encryption  
- Compliance with PDPL requirements  
- Transfers only to countries or entities providing acceptable protection levels  

---

#### Data Security

We apply industry-standard security practices, including:

- Encryption in transit and at rest  
- Access controls and authentication  
- Monitoring for suspicious activity  
- Secure development and DevSecOps methods  

While we take strong security measures, no system is completely secure.

---

#### Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:

- Fulfill the purpose for which it was collected  
- Meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations  

Afterward, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

---

#### Your Rights Under PDPL

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data  
- **Request correction** of inaccurate or incomplete data  
- **Request deletion** of your personal data  
- **Withdraw consent** where applicable  
- **Understand how your data is processed**  

To exercise these rights, contact us at [info@neurodynamic.sa](mailto:info@neurodynamic.sa)
